Before you step in

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Starting something new comes with questions. We're here to help you feel ready.

Ask us anything
Do I need any experience?

No. Complete beginners are welcome. Let us know it's your first class and the coaches will help you get started. You don't need to get in shape before reaching out.

What is no gi jiu jitsu?

Jiu jitsu is a grappling sport built around takedowns, control, escapes, and submissions. No gi means you train without the traditional kimono, usually in shorts and a rash guard. It focuses on grappling rather than punches or kicks.

What should I bring to my first class?

Wear clean athletic shorts without pockets or zippers and a rash guard or fitted athletic shirt. Bring water. You don't need to buy a gi. Contact us beforehand so a coach can help you prepare.

How much does it cost?

Your first class is free. For current membership prices and options, call (603) 459 3610 or email precisionjiujitsu29@gmail.com. We'll help you choose based on the classes you want to attend.

Can my child try a class? What ages can join?

Kids classes welcome children ages 5 and up. Tell us your child's age and experience so a coach can confirm the right class. Kids sessions run Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday from 4:30 to 5:30 PM.

Can I visit from another academy?

All affiliations are welcome. Our published schedule includes open mats and competition training. Please contact us before your visit to confirm the session and any visitor details.
